      How these numbers are calculated

      Two poles. Everything here is built from official MLB season lines. The FIP pole credits a pitcher only for what he controls without his defense — strikeouts, walks, hit batters, homers: FIP = (13·HR + 3·(BB+HBP) − 2·K)/IP plus a league constant, moved onto the runs-allowed scale. The RA9 pole charges him with every run he allowed, earned or not. The 50/50 column and the manual slider are straight linear blends of the two.

      The defense adjustment. Baseball-Reference shifts each pitcher's runs-allowed context by the quality of the defense behind him. We run the same mechanism with public data: Statcast team Outs Above Average, converted at 0.8 runs per out, allocated to each pitcher by his share of his team's balls in play — stint by stint for traded players. Good defense behind you means runs get added back to your RA9, which lowers your WAR.

      Shared skeleton. League constants come from every pitcher to throw a pitch this season. Replacement level blends starter and reliever rates (.380/.470 win%) by each pitcher's GS/G. Runs-per-win is 1.5 × league RA9 + 3. WAR = (replacement RA9 − pitcher's rate) / runs-per-win × IP/9.

      Why totals differ from FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference. fWAR adds park factors, league adjustments, infield flies in FIP, a dynamic runs-per-win, and a leverage bump for relievers — none of which are here. bWAR uses licensed DRS, which includes throwing arms and catcher defense where OAA measures range only, so our defense corrections run smaller than theirs; bWAR also adjusts for park and opponent quality. Expect rankings to track closely and totals to drift by a few tenths of a win.

      Data. MLB Stats API season pitching lines and Baseball Savant team fielding, rebuilt every morning.
